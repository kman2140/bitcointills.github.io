A little more clarity

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Getting started should feel straightforward. Here's what to expect.

Do I need to know how Bitcoin works?

No. We help you get set up and show you the payment workflow. The aim is to give you and your team a clear, practical way to accept payments.

Can I use my existing point of sale?

We'll review your existing POS and check compatibility with the payment setup. If a different configuration or cradle fit is needed, we'll discuss it with you before getting started.

What does the A$199 include?

A$199 covers your original BitcoinTills POS unit. This is followed by the A$20/month support subscription, which includes all six services listed above. A cold-storage device and any additional equipment are separate.

Will you hold my Bitcoin or recovery words?

No. This is a setup and support service. Your wallet and recovery words stay with you. Never put recovery words, private keys or wallet credentials into the enquiry form.

What does the free advertising include?

Business promotion is included in your membership. We'll agree on the business details and material to use during onboarding. Help Bitcoin customers discover your business, including overseas travellers who seek out places to spend their Bitcoin and may make a special trip to visit.

How does the subscription start?

Send a setup enquiry and we'll confirm the details with you. The original POS unit is A$199, followed by A$20 per month for the subscription, all in Australian dollars. This website currently takes setup enquiries; it does not collect payments or activate automatic billing.

Is the A$20 subscription a transaction fee?

No. It's the monthly price for setup and ongoing support services. Any wallet, Lightning network, on-chain or third-party provider fees are separate and depend on your chosen setup.
